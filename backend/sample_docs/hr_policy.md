# ACME Corporation — Human Resources Policy Manual
## Version 3.2 | Effective Date: January 1, 2025

---

## 1. INTRODUCTION AND SCOPE

This Human Resources Policy Manual applies to all full-time, part-time, and contract employees of ACME Corporation and its subsidiaries. These policies are designed to promote a fair, respectful, and productive work environment. Employees are expected to read, understand, and comply with all policies herein.

For questions not addressed in this manual, employees should contact the HR Department at hr@acme.com or call the HR Helpdesk at ext. 4500 (available Monday–Friday, 9 AM – 5 PM EST).

---

## 2. EMPLOYMENT POLICIES

### 2.1 Equal Opportunity Employment
ACME Corporation is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, genetic information, veteran status, sexual orientation, or gender identity. All employment decisions are made solely on the basis of qualifications, merit, and business needs.

### 2.2 At-Will Employment
Unless otherwise specified in a written employment contract, all employment at ACME Corporation is at-will. Either the employee or the company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, for any lawful reason, with or without notice.

### 2.3 Background Checks
All new hires are subject to a pre-employment background check, including criminal history, employment verification, and education verification. Employment offers are contingent upon satisfactory completion of the background check.

### 2.4 Probationary Period
New employees serve a 90-day probationary period. During this time, performance is closely monitored. At the end of the probationary period, a formal review is conducted to determine continued employment.

## 3. COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

### 3.1 Pay Schedule
Employees are paid bi-weekly (every two weeks) via direct deposit. Payday falls on every other Friday. If payday falls on a holiday, payment will be made on the preceding business day.

### 3.2 Overtime Policy
Non-exempt employees who work more than 40 hours in a workweek are entitled to overtime pay at 1.5 times their regular hourly rate. All overtime must be pre-approved by the employee's direct manager. Unapproved overtime may result in disciplinary action.

### 3.3 Health Insurance
ACME Corporation provides comprehensive health insurance coverage including medical, dental, and vision plans. Coverage begins on the first day of the month following 30 days of employment. Employees may enroll dependents (spouse and children under 26) during the open enrollment period or within 30 days of a qualifying life event.

### 3.4 Retirement Plan (401k)
Employees are eligible to participate in the company's 401(k) plan after 90 days of employment. ACME matches 100% of employee contributions up to 4% of annual salary. Vesting for company match is on a 3-year graded schedule: 33% after year 1, 66% after year 2, 100% after year 3.

### 3.5 Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP)
Eligible employees may purchase ACME Corporation stock at a 15% discount through payroll deductions during designated offering periods. Participation is voluntary and open to all full-time employees after 6 months of employment.

## 4. LEAVE POLICIES

### 4.1 Paid Time Off (PTO)
PTO is accrued based on years of service:
- Years 0–2: 15 days per year (1.25 days/month)
- Years 3–5: 20 days per year (1.67 days/month)
- Years 6+: 25 days per year (2.08 days/month)

PTO may be used for vacation, personal days, or sick leave. Unused PTO up to 10 days may be carried over to the following calendar year. PTO in excess of 10 days is forfeited at year-end.

### 4.2 Holidays
ACME Corporation observes 11 paid holidays per year:
- New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day
- Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day, Day after Thanksgiving
- Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year's Eve

### 4.3 Family and Medical Leave (FMLA)
Eligible employees (12+ months of service, 1,250+ hours worked in past year) may take up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave per year for qualifying family or medical reasons. To initiate FMLA leave, contact HR at least 30 days in advance when the need is foreseeable.

### 4.4 Parental Leave
ACME Corporation provides:
- Primary caregivers: 16 weeks of paid parental leave
- Secondary caregivers: 4 weeks of paid parental leave
Leave must be taken within 12 months of the child's birth, adoption, or foster placement.

### 4.5 Bereavement Leave
Employees receive up to 5 paid days off for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, grandparent). Up to 3 paid days for other close relatives. Additional unpaid leave may be requested and is subject to manager approval.

## 5. WORKPLACE CONDUCT

### 5.1 Code of Conduct
All employees are expected to conduct themselves professionally and ethically at all times. This includes treating colleagues, customers, and vendors with respect, maintaining confidentiality of sensitive information, avoiding conflicts of interest, and complying with all applicable laws and regulations.

### 5.2 Anti-Harassment Policy
ACME Corporation has a zero-tolerance policy for harassment of any kind, including sexual harassment, verbal abuse, intimidation, or hostile work environment behavior. Employees who experience or witness harassment should report it immediately to HR or use the anonymous Ethics Hotline at 1-800-555-ETHICS.

### 5.3 Drug and Alcohol Policy
Employees must report to work free from the influence of alcohol, illegal drugs, or any substance that impairs their ability to perform their duties safely and effectively. Violation of this policy may result in immediate termination.

### 5.4 Disciplinary Process
ACME uses a progressive disciplinary approach:
1. Verbal warning (documented)
2. Written warning
3. Final written warning / Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
4. Termination

Serious violations (fraud, harassment, violence, theft) may result in immediate termination without prior steps.

## 6. PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT

### 6.1 Annual Performance Reviews
Performance reviews are conducted annually in December. Employees are assessed on goal achievement, core competencies, and behavioral standards. Reviews directly influence merit increase decisions effective January 1 each year.

### 6.2 Merit Increases
Merit increases are budgeted annually. Typical ranges:
- Exceeds Expectations: 4–6% increase
- Meets Expectations: 2–3% increase
- Below Expectations: 0% (may trigger PIP)

### 6.3 Goal Setting
Employees and managers must collaboratively set SMART goals (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) at the start of each year. Mid-year check-ins are required.

---

## 7. REMOTE WORK POLICY

### 7.1 Eligibility
Remote work is available to employees whose roles can be performed effectively outside the office, at manager discretion. Employees must be in good standing (no active PIPs or disciplinary actions).

### 7.2 Hybrid Schedule
The standard hybrid model is 3 days in-office, 2 days remote per week. Specific in-office days are determined by team and manager.

### 7.3 Equipment and Security
ACME provides laptops and necessary peripherals for remote work. Employees must use company-approved VPN when accessing internal systems. Use of personal devices for company data is prohibited without IT approval.
