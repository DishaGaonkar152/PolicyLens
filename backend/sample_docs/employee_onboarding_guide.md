# ACME Corporation — New Employee Onboarding Guide
## Welcome to ACME! | HR-OB-2025 | Effective: January 2025

---

## WELCOME MESSAGE

Congratulations and welcome to the ACME Corporation family! We're thrilled to have you on board. This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to get settled in during your first 90 days. Our goal is to make your transition as smooth and welcoming as possible.

If at any point you have questions that aren't answered here, please reach out to your assigned Onboarding Buddy, your manager, or the HR team at hr@acme.com.

---

## SECTION 1: BEFORE YOUR FIRST DAY

### 1.1 Pre-Employment Checklist
Before you arrive, please complete the following:

**Required Documents (submit to HR within 3 days of offer acceptance):**
- Completed I-9 Employment Eligibility Verification form
- Copy of government-issued photo ID + work authorization documents
- Direct deposit authorization form (for payroll)
- Emergency contact form
- Signed offer letter and NDA

**Account Setup (IT will email you setup instructions):**
- Activate your ACME email account (firstname.lastname@acme.com)
- Set up Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) on your phone
- Complete cybersecurity awareness training (link sent to personal email)

### 1.2 What to Bring on Day One
- Government-issued photo ID (required for building access badge)
- Any remaining onboarding paperwork
- Personal items for your workspace (optional)

## SECTION 2: YOUR FIRST DAY

### 2.1 Arrival and Check-In
Report to the main reception at 9:00 AM on your start date. The receptionist will direct you to HR for your welcome meeting. Parking is available in Garage B — your parking validation will be provided during orientation.

**Office Address:** 1200 Innovation Drive, Suite 500, Austin, TX 78701

### 2.2 Day One Agenda
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 9:00–10:00 AM | Welcome meeting with HR — paperwork, badge photo |
| 10:00–11:00 AM | IT setup — laptop provisioning, account access |
| 11:00–12:00 PM | Office tour with your Onboarding Buddy |
| 12:00–1:00 PM | Welcome lunch with your team (manager's arrangement) |
| 1:00–3:00 PM | Benefits enrollment orientation (HR) |
| 3:00–5:00 PM | Department introduction and manager 1:1 |

### 2.3 Building Access
You will receive your proximity card access badge on Day 1. The badge grants access to:
- Main office floors (your assigned floor and common areas)
- Parking Garage B
- Fitness center (Building C, Level B1)
- Cafeteria (open 7 AM–3 PM)

For access to restricted areas (server rooms, finance floor, executive suites), submit an Access Request via the IT Portal.

## SECTION 3: FIRST WEEK ACTIVITIES

### 3.1 Mandatory Training Modules
All new employees must complete the following eLearning modules within the first 5 business days. Access via LMS at https://learn.acme.com:

1. **Code of Conduct & Ethics** (45 min) — Required for compliance
2. **Information Security Awareness** (60 min) — Required for system access continuation
3. **Anti-Harassment & Discrimination Prevention** (30 min) — Required by law
4. **Workplace Safety Overview** (20 min) — Required for office access
5. **Data Privacy & GDPR Basics** (30 min) — Required for all employees handling customer data

Failure to complete mandatory training within 5 business days may result in temporary system access suspension.

### 3.2 System Access and Tools Setup
Your IT provisioning ticket will include access to:

**Communication & Collaboration:**
- Microsoft Teams (primary communication tool)
- Outlook 365 (corporate email)
- SharePoint (document management)
- Zoom (external video calls)

**Business Applications (role-dependent):**
- Salesforce CRM (Sales, Account Management)
- Jira & Confluence (Engineering, Product)
- Workday (HR self-service — all employees)
- SAP (Finance, Operations)

If you need access to additional systems, submit an Access Request Form via the IT Self-Service Portal (https://itportal.acme.com).

### 3.3 Meeting Your Onboarding Buddy
You will be paired with an Onboarding Buddy — an experienced colleague from your department. Your buddy will:
- Answer informal questions about company culture
- Introduce you to key stakeholders
- Share tips on tools and processes
- Check in weekly during your first 90 days

Reach out to your buddy proactively! Schedule 30-minute weekly check-ins.

## SECTION 4: FIRST 30 DAYS — LEARNING PHASE

### 4.1 Goals for Days 1–30
- Complete all mandatory training ✓
- Understand your team's structure, goals, and current projects
- Schedule 1:1 meetings with all immediate team members
- Shadow key team processes and workflows
- Set up all required software and tools
- Attend at least 2 company-wide All-Hands meetings or recordings

### 4.2 Key Policies to Review
During your first 30 days, review these critical company policies on the HR Portal (https://hr.acme.com):
- Remote Work & Hybrid Policy
- Expense Reimbursement Policy
- Travel Policy
- Time & Attendance Policy
- Intellectual Property Agreement

### 4.3 Expense Reimbursement for New Hires
New hire expenses (relocation, home office setup) follow these guidelines:
- Relocation allowance: Up to \$5,000 for moves over 50 miles (pre-approval required)
- Home office equipment: Up to \$500 one-time (laptop stand, keyboard, monitor — IT approved)
- Submit expenses via Concur expense management system within 30 days of incurring the expense

---

## SECTION 5: DAYS 31–90 — CONTRIBUTION PHASE

### 5.1 Goals for Days 31–60
- Take ownership of at least one independent project or task area
- Contribute meaningfully to team meetings and discussions
- Complete first informal check-in with your manager (mid-probation review)
- Identify areas for professional development

### 5.2 Goals for Days 61–90
- Demonstrate measurable contributions aligned to team goals
- Begin work on formal SMART goals for the performance year
- Complete 90-Day Probationary Review with manager
- Provide feedback via the Onboarding Experience Survey

### 5.3 90-Day Probationary Review
At the end of 90 days, your manager will conduct a formal review covering:
- Performance against initial goals and expectations
- Core competency assessment
- Cultural fit and team integration
- Confirmation of continued employment and transition to regular status

You will also have an opportunity to share your own feedback on the onboarding experience.
